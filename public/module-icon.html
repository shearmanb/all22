Module Icons

A fixed identity layer: every module owns a permanent hue that never re-themes per team. The team theme owns chrome, nav and the active state; module hues are wayfinding only — tinted duotone chips, never a background fill.

App bar & active state — owned by the team, not the hue

On the team-primary app bar the glyph goes mono in --abi. The selected item is owned by the team accent --pop (faint pill + underline + --pop glyph) — it does not switch to the module's bright hue. Both track the team you pick in the nav — try the picker and the 🎲.
