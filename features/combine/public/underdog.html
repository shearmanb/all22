Underdog Upload weekly quick path

Underdog only accepts its own file, carrying its own player IDs — and those IDs change every contest and every week. Give it Underdog's download plus any rankings file; you get Underdog's file back, reordered to the rankings, ready to upload.

1. Underdog's CSV

On Underdog's Rankings page for the contest, tap CSV download. Saved files are kept here, so you can download once on a laptop and build from your phone.

2. The rankings to upload

A Fantasy Points weekly export, a FantasyPros CSV, Scott Barrett's sheet, any spreadsheet with a player-name column — or paste a list, one player per line. Columns are detected from the header.

      These ranked players have no row in the Underdog file, so they can't be uploaded. Expect a lot of them: kickers and defenses (Underdog's contests carry neither) and every player whose team isn't in this contest. Where Underdog has someone this could be — a shared last name, or the same position on the same team — pick him and tap Same player. That pairing is remembered in the app's player list and applies every week from then on, so you only ever teach a name once.
