Underdog Upload weekly quick path

Underdog's uploader only accepts its own file, with its own per-contest player IDs — and those IDs change every contest and every week. Give it Underdog's download plus any rankings file; you get Underdog's file back, reordered to the rankings, ready to upload. Nothing is saved.

1. Underdog's CSV

On Underdog's Rankings page for the contest, click CSV download. Season-long contests list ~1,400 players; a weekly slate lists only the teams playing.

2. The rankings to upload

A FantasyPros weekly CSV export, Scott Barrett's sheet, any spreadsheet with a player-name column — or paste a list, one player per line. Columns are detected from the header.
